LifeDrop Daily - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Akash Pol
Contact: akpolstudio@gmail.com
App: LifeDrop Daily (package name: com.lifedropdaily.app)

This Privacy Policy explains what information LifeDrop Daily ("the app") handles and how it is used. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • No account, email address or phone number is needed to use the app.
  • Your journal, answers, favorites, XP, streak, custom events and settings are stored on your device. The developer does not receive or see this data.
  • You can optionally back this data up to a file you choose, or let Android keep an automatic backup in your own Google Account (see section 3).
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Pro removes all ads.
  • Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your phone:

  • Journal entries and tags
  • Your answers to daily questions and challenges
  • Favorites, XP, streak, badges and progress
  • Custom events and dates you add
  • App settings, such as theme, font and reminder choices

This data is not uploaded to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. The developer never receives a copy. Optional backups are described in section 3. You can export your journal, delete your journal, or reset all progress and data at any time in Settings > Privacy. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

3. Backup and restore

The app offers two optional ways to back up your data.

  • Backup file: in Settings > Backup & Restore you can save a backup file that contains your streak, XP, drops, journal, answers, favorites, custom events and settings. You choose where the file is saved, for example Google Drive, your Downloads folder or another app. The developer does not receive this file. To restore, you select the file in the app. Anyone who has the file can read your journal, so keep it somewhere private.
  • Automatic backup: when this is turned on (it is on by default and can be turned off in Settings > Backup & Restore), the app keeps a small backup copy of the same data in its private storage on your phone. If Google backup is enabled on your device, Android may save this copy to your Google Account's backup (Google Drive) and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is done by Android and Google, not by the developer, and is covered by Google's terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot access it. Turning automatic backup off deletes the copy on your phone. To remove a copy that Google already stores, manage your device backups in your Google Account settings.

Backups do not include your Pro purchase, which belongs to your Google account and can be restored from Google Play. Restoring a backup replaces the progress, journal and events currently on the phone.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, files, microphone or camera data. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services run by the developer.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: for daily reminders, event, festival and sky alerts, and the optional day-count notification. You can turn each of these off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only to show ads in the free version and to connect to Google Play for purchases.
  • Receive boot completed: lets the app restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

6. Advertising (free version)

The free version of the app shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google, not by the developer. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob.

You can reset your advertising ID, turn off ad personalization, or opt out of interest-based ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). You can also remove all ads by buying Pro.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

7. Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. The app does not see or store your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro, so that it can unlock Pro features and remove ads. You can restore a past purchase on a new device using the same Google account.

8. Sharing

The app does not sell or share your personal data. Content leaves the app only when you choose to share it, for example by tapping Share, exporting your journal or saving a backup file. You choose which app receives it. Information handled by Google AdMob and Google Play is covered by Google's own policies.

9. Data security and retention

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Data is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Backup files you save, and backup copies that Android stores in your Google Account, are outside the control of the app once saved, so protect your Google Account and keep backup files private. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device protected with a screen lock.

10. Your choices and rights

You can view, export, back up, restore, delete or reset your data in the app at any time, turn off automatic backup, turn off notifications, and control ad personalization in your device settings. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For any privacy request or question, contact us at the email below.

11. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same web address with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
